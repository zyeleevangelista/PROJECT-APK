# Privacy Policy — Lock Video Recorder Premium

**Last updated: October 27, 2026**

Lock Video Recorder Premium is an Android app for user-initiated video recording. The app is designed to store video recordings on your Android device. The app developer does not operate a server for this app.

## Camera and microphone access

The app requests access to your **camera** to preview and record video. **Microphone** access is optional and is requested only if you enable audio recording. Video and audio may contain personal or sensitive information, depending on what you choose to record. You control when recording starts and stops. You must start recording while the app is open and your phone is unlocked. A foreground recording notification remains visible during recording, including while the screen is locked on supported devices. Android may also display camera and microphone access indicators.

## Storage, data use and sharing

Recordings are saved to your device's shared Movies/LockVideoRecorder folder as MP4 files. You can access, share or delete them using your device's Gallery or Files application. Recordings are **not uploaded** by this app or sent to the developer. There is no app account, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, cloud backup service or networking permission in the Android application. The app does not sell or share your video, audio, or other personal data with advertisers.

The Android operating system and any third-party app you choose to use to view, share, sync or back up your recordings have their own privacy practices, outside this app's control.

## Retention and deletion

Saved recordings remain on your device until you delete them, including after uninstalling the application when Android retains files in shared media storage. Delete recorded videos in your device's Gallery or Files application. Videos that fail to record are removed when the app can clean them up. The app holds no recordings in developer-managed cloud storage.

## Payments

If this app is offered as a paid Google Play download, purchases are handled by Google Play; the app itself does not receive your payment-card details. Google's own policies govern purchase processing.

## Security

Recordings are stored in Android shared media storage, where other apps with appropriate permissions may access them. Use your device's screen lock and Android security settings to help protect recordings. Record only where permitted by applicable laws and with any required consent.

## Changes and questions

We may update this policy when app functionality or privacy practices change. Questions about privacy may be directed to the developer through the contact information on the Google Play store listing or the project's GitHub Issues page: https://github.com/zyeleevangelista/PROJECT-APK/issues. Do not post personal recordings or sensitive details in a public issue.
